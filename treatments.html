  • Highly cost-effective aesthetic fix

Custom Ceramic & Zirconia Crowns

  • 3D optical scanning — no putty impressions
  • Color-matched to adjacent dentition

Fixed Dental Bridges

Fixed non-removable ceramic units designed to replace one or more missing consecutive teeth by anchoring onto adjacent stable natural teeth.

  • Restores full chewing stability
  • Prevents adjacent teeth from drifting
  • Precision-milled framework
Consult on Bridges

High-comfort partial or complete prosthetic solutions designed with flexible, lightweight materials that conform closely to natural gum contours.

  • Implant-supported overdenture compatability

  • Guided surgical template placement

  • Lifetime function with proper hygiene
