The Aurelia Method

Dentistry Designed Around You.

We have eliminated the rush, the pressure, and the cold clinical atmosphere of traditional dental offices. Every visit follows a structured, collaborative pathway.

01

Listen

We begin each visit seated in a private consultation lounge to understand your oral concerns, lifestyle, aesthetic aspirations, and any past dental anxiety.

02

Assess

Comprehensive digital examination using low-dose radiography and 3D intraoral imaging — thoroughly checking enamel, gums, bite dynamics, and jaw joint health.

03

Plan

We co-create a clear, transparent treatment roadmap. You view digital simulations, understand all available options, timelines, and costs before proceeding.

04

Treat

Care is delivered with precision instrumentation, magnification, and gentle anesthetic protocols designed to maximize your comfort at every minute.

05

Follow Up

Our commitment extends beyond your appointment with proactive recovery checks, preventative maintenance reminders, and ongoing smile care.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your visit, our care standards, and appointment procedures.

How should I prepare for my first visit?
Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to enjoy a refreshment in our lounge. If you have recent dental records, digital X-rays from another practice, or a list of current medications, you are welcome to bring them or share them beforehand. We begin with a calm seated conversation about your goals rather than an immediate invasive examination.
How often should I schedule a dental checkup?
For most adults and children with stable oral health, we recommend a routine comprehensive checkup and gentle hygiene cleaning every 6 months. Patients managing periodontal conditions, active orthodontic aligners, or extensive cosmetic restorations may benefit from tailored 3 to 4-month maintenance intervals.
Do you offer clear aligners?
Yes. Our orthodontics lead, Dr. Rohan Shah, provides modern clear aligner solutions for teenagers and adults. Using 3D optical scanning, we can simulate your tooth movement step by step before ordering your custom, virtually invisible trays.
What happens during a consultation?
During a consultation, you sit comfortably with your dentist to discuss your aesthetic hopes, functional concerns, and past dental experiences. We take digital photographs and low-radiation scans if needed, explain your current oral baseline, and present transparent treatment pathways with no pressure to decide on the spot.
How long does a typical appointment take?
Initial consultations and comprehensive examinations typically take between 45 and 60 minutes. Routine maintenance and cleaning visits generally take approximately 45 minutes. Because we do not double-book slots, your appointment starts promptly at your scheduled time.
Do you treat children?
Yes, our studio welcomes patients of all ages, including young children. Our team takes special care to introduce children to the dental environment with gentle, friendly pacing, interactive explanations, and positive reinforcement to ensure they grow up free from dental fear.
How do I request an appointment?
You can request an appointment using our online booking form above, by calling our clinic directly at +91 22 5550 1928, or by sending a quick message via WhatsApp. Our clinic coordinator will get back to you promptly to confirm your chosen slot.
